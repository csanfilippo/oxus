# Increment 14: search by camera and lens - Test List

## Specifications
- Tool: `search` gains `camera` and `lens` (strings, optional)
- `camera`: each whitespace-separated word must appear in kMDItemAcquisitionMake or kMDItemAcquisitionModel, ignoring case and diacritics
- `lens`: each whitespace-separated word must appear in kMDItemLensModel, ignoring case and diacritics
- At least one of name_contains, kind, folder, camera and lens is required; filters in the same call are combined with AND
- Blank or non-string camera or lens: result with isError, same message as the other non-empty string arguments
- Quotes, backslashes and asterisks in a word are escaped like name_contains
- Result, limit, truncated, unreadableFolders and folder errors unchanged; spotlight_query unchanged
- Spotlight port and adapter unchanged
- Checked on 2026-11-19 with mdls on a Fujifilm X-T4 RAF: make "FUJIFILM", model "X-T4", lens "XF16-80mmF4 R OIS WR"
